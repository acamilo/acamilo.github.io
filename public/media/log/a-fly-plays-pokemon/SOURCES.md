# Image sources for “A fly plays Pokémon, Part 1”

These are local, compressed editorial illustrations of the projects discussed
in the post. Credits also appear in the post's visible figure captions. The
original works retain their respective copyrights; no blanket license is claimed.

## male-fly-connectome.webp

- Source article: https://research.google/blog/a-connectomics-milestone-mapping-the-complete-male-fruit-fly-brain/
- Source image: https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-research2023-media/images/Male-Fruit-Fly-Brain-Map-1.width-1250.png
- Credit: Google Research; connectome work by HHMI Janelia, the Cambridge
  Connectomics Group, Google Research, and collaborators.
- Description: Two views of a subset of reconstructed cells in the male fruit
  fly brain and ventral nerve cord. This is not an image of the Pokémon simulation.
- Processing: Converted to WebP; no crop or compositional changes.

## infinite-sugar.webp

- Source application: https://infinitesugar.cnqso.com/
- Creator/source code: William Kelly (cnqso), https://github.com/cnqso/infinite-sugar
- Description: Browser screenshot of the simulated fly, neural activity display,
  feeding-signal counter, and Sugar on control, with the About overlay closed.
- The application's credits include FlyWire (connectome data, CC BY-NC 4.0),
  FlyBody, MuJoCo, and scenery assets credited individually in its About panel.
  These are underlying project credits, not a license for the entire screenshot.
- Processing: Screenshot resized from 1440×1000 to 1280×889 and converted to WebP.
